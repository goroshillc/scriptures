The King James Bible with the master engravings, free and offline, for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Dedicated to JESUS CHRIST, who is God; all of it is from Him (John 15:5).

**Install**

- Windows 10 and 11: `Scriptures_*_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`). On the SmartScreen notice choose More info, then Run anyway.
- macOS 11 or newer: `Scriptures_*_universal.dmg`. On first launch, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, then Open Anyway.
- Linux: the `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`. Arch Linux: the attached `PKGBUILD` (`makepkg -si`).

The installers carry every page and plate (about half a gigabyte), so nothing is downloaded later. The app opens no port and makes no network request.

**Wallpapers**: `scriptures-wallpapers-<size>.zip`, every plate centred on white, numbered in the order of the Bible. Omarchy instructions are in the README and in each folder.
