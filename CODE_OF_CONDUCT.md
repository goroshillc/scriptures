# Code of Conduct

This project serves the King James Bible to anyone who wants to read it. Everyone who takes part,
in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project, is asked to act in the
spirit of the book it serves.

> *"Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them:
> for this is the law and the prophets."* - Matthew 7:12, KJV

## What we ask

- **Be kind and patient.** *"Charity suffereth long, and is kind"* (1 Corinthians 13:4).
- **Speak the truth in love** (Ephesians 4:15): disagree with the work, never attack the person.
- **Be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath** (James 1:19).
- **Respect every person of every nation.** *"And hath made of one blood all nations of men for to
  dwell on all the face of the earth"* (Acts 17:26). Contempt for anyone for their people, nation,
  race or tradition has no place here.
- **Keep to the work.** Questions of doctrine are welcome where they bear on the reader, discussed
  charitably; this is not the place for arguments between churches.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Hatred or mockery of any people, nation or race, including antisemitism.
- Sexual, obscene or violent content.
- Publishing anyone's private information (addresses, phone numbers, emails) without permission.
- Spam and self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## When something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab
(*Report a vulnerability* reaches the maintainers only), or use GitHub's *Report content*.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block anyone who does not follow this
code. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

> *"If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men."* - Romans 12:18, KJV
